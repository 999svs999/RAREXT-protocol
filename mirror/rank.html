What this does not say

It does not say RAREXT writes better prose, knows more, reads images, or holds a long conversation. Those are the loss rows, and they are real. RAREXT's claim is narrower and checkable: when the answer must be exact, private, free, offline and bound to the bytes that produced it, it is the best of the 100. When the task needs breadth, it is not in the running — and this page says so on the same screen as the wins.
